# Water

How water works now: the block, how it's drawn, the sea and lakes, swimming, the view under it, and how it's tested.

## The block

- `water` is a realm block (gam/zoxel/sys/blocks.c, `BlockWater`): `BlockMaterial` liquid, `BlockCollider` air
  (walked through), `BlockLightPass` (light goes through it).
- A liquid is a chunk material of its own (chunk materials, src/chunks/chunks3/fun/materials.c): its faces draw only
  against air and other see through materials that aren't water, so there are no faces between water blocks or
  against solids, and a solid keeps its face against water (the sea floor shows through).
- Its chunk meshes are tagged `TransparentMesh` and drawn by `chunk3_transparent_render_system`, after the solid
  ones and the sky, blended, both faces (src/rendering/rendering3/sys/render_chunks.c), far to near from the camera
  (`render_chunk_order`, within a table) so water seen through water blends over it.

## Its texture

- `prefab_texture_water` (src/textures/pre/noise_texture.c), generated by `water_texture_system`: 16 frames of
  ripples (src/textures/gen/water.c), three waves with whole wave numbers across it and whole cycles over the frames,
  so it tiles and loops; a few shades, the crests brightest.
- Not on the tilemap: liquid blocks are left out of it (blocks/sys/tilemap.c). Their chunk meshes link the liquid's
  texture (`MeshTextureLink`), each face spanning the whole texture, and the see through pass binds it.
- It has its own gpu texture; `texture_animate_system` copies the frame for the time (`TextureFrameRate`, 8 a second)
  into its `TextureData` and uploads just it (on the tilemap, the whole tilemap was uploaded 8 times a second).
- Its size is set when its block is made (`spawn_realm_block_textured`), a block texture's size then (the Block Res
  setting resizes the block texture prefabs only; on the tilemap at Block Res 5 it filled a quarter of its tile,
  tst water_tile, run with zox_tst_settings=~/.config/zoxel/settings.cfg).

## The sea

- Ground below `terrain_sea_height` (6, times `render_distance_y`: 18) is under water up to it
  (src/heights/sys/landfill.c). The land is mostly 17 to 30 high, so about a tenth is under it; sand reaches 21 for
  its shores. Vegetation never grows under 21.

## Lakes

- src/heights/fun/_.c: a 64 block cell (`zox_lake_cell`) holds a lake 30% of the time, seeded from the terrain's
  seed: a centre, a radius of 8 to 15, a depth of 3 to 5. Its bowl is carved into the heights
  (`terrain_height_at` = `terrain_height_base` less `terrain_lake_dip`), deepest in the middle.
- Its level is a block under its rim's lowest point (16 samples of the land before carving), so it stays in its bowl;
  one under the sea is just a dip the sea fills.
- Landfill fills water to the higher of the sea and the column's lake (each lake's level worked out once a chunk).
- Nothing grows in a bowl (vegetation skips its columns); towns and mountains keep out of lakes
  (`region_zones_add_lakes` adds them to a region's placement zones before those are placed).

## Light

- A sunbeam loses `light_water_decay` (24, lights3/set) in each liquid block it passes (`light_dim`, lights3/fun/
  solidity.c, the liquids table `update_light_liquids` beside solidity): full sunlight in air over it, 231 in the top
  water, 207 under it, and so on to `darklight`. A lake's floor is darker than its beach.
- There's no beam marker: a beam is known by its light. `light_beam_above` walks up a column (into the chunk above
  too) while each voxel's light is what a beam would give it, until full sunlight; edits use it to find the beam over
  them (voxel_light_system, editing.c).
- `dark_sunbeam` (dark.c) carries the light it expects down the column (dimmed in each liquid) and darkens while it
  matches; each voxel's dark flood clears light a step dimmer than that voxel had, so a beam beside it as deep in
  water stays.
- Water placed in a beam (flowing too): a `zox_light_type_dim_start` dark beam from it down, then each voxel relit a
  liquid dimmer (a flood at its new light); it carries on into the chunk below (`zox_light_type_dim`). Water taken
  out (drained): a sunbeam from it down at the beam over it, brighter.
- A chunk's beams wait until it's generated (`GenerateChunk` before `zox_generate_terrain_sunlight`): the top
  chunk's beams went down into chunks still empty and lit them as air, their water too.
- Tests: `water_light` (a pool filled a layer at a time, drained, covered: exact values), `lakes` (a generated
  lake's floor darker than its surface, darker than the air over it).

## Flowing

- Levels, Minecraft style: `water` (BlockWater) is a source, `BlockWaterLevel` 8, it never moves (the sea, lakes,
  placed water). Flowing water is a block a level, `water 7` to `water 1` (BlockWaterFlowing, gam/zoxel/sys/
  blocks.c, after water so the blocks before keep their indices): no texture of their own (the see through mesh
  binds the first liquid's, water's), no items, they drop nothing.
- `WaterLevels` (terrain/fun/water.c): a realm's water voxels by level and levels by voxel; `voxel_is_water`.
  Swimming, the splash and the view under water take any level (`terrain_water_at`: a flowing block only up to
  its level, unless water is over it).
- src/terrain/sys/water_flow.c: a block's water is what flows into it: from water over it 7 (it falls); else from
  the water beside it that lies on something (not air, not flowing), a level less (a source gives 7). Falling water
  doesn't spread sideways, only where it lands.
- A chunk just edited (`VoxelNodePostDirty`) adds its edited blocks and the six round each to its terrain's
  `WaterFlows`; `water_flow_system` takes up to 64 due a frame: a block of air or flowing water takes the level
  flowing into it (filling, rising, or lowering), and when it changed the blocks round it are checked a step
  (`zox_water_flow_time`, a quarter second) later. A block already waiting waits till the later time (else it was
  checked before the change round it landed, and lost).
- Draining: water with nothing feeding it drops to what its neighbours give it, a level less each step, until it's
  gone. A source taken out drains all it fed; a lake (sources) never drains, dug out of its side it fills the hole
  and spreads 7 into a tunnel.
- Flowing water's own edits are marked (`VoxelNodeUpdate.flowed`): they start no flows of their own and drop nothing.
- Drawn thinner (chunks3/sys/build_textured.c, `heights` from BlockWaterLevel): a flowing block's top face and its
  sides' tops at level / 8 of a block, unless water is over it (no top face, drawn full). The gpu mesher (a proof
  of concept, off) does the same from `gpu_mesher_set_heights` (tst gpu_mesh).
- Generated water (landfill) doesn't flow: only edits do.

## Swimming

- `InWater` (a tag, src/terrain/com): added and removed with the splash (collisions/eve/water_splash.c), when the
  body's `InsideBlock` (its lower body, inside.c) goes into or out of water.
- `swim_system` (src/characters/jump/sys/swim.c), in water: holds up 85% of its gravity (it sinks slowly, about 0.4m
  a second) and drags its velocity (3 a second: a fall into water slows softly); a jump is a stroke up
  (`jump_state_swim`, 12m/s/s, about 3.5m a second at most), held it swims up to the surface and bobs there.
- `jump_system`: in water it can always "jump" (swim); grounded on the bottom it jumps as on land; at the surface
  with a solid block beside the air over it (`swim_at_shore`) it jumps out, onto the shore.
- Walking in water is half speed (`swim_speed`, player3d_move_system and the XR direct move).

## Under water

- `camera_underwater_system` (src/terrain/sys/water_view.c): a 3D camera in a water block (`terrain_water_at`) gets
  `Underwater` (src/cameras/com). XR's eyes check their own in xr/fun/eye_render.c.
- `camera_render3_system` sets `render_underwater` for the camera it draws: `get_fog_value` turns the fog blue and
  thick (src/rendering/dat/camera_renderer.c, fully blue about 9 blocks out); skybox.frag covers the sky with a thick
  fog too (so the sky doesn't show through the water).
- A camera drawn through its render texture (the game's, not viewports or XR eyes) eases its `CameraTint` to 1
  under water and back to 0 out of it (about a third of a second): res/shaders/render_texture_rgb.frag soaks up
  reds and greens, deep blue (tst underwater: the player's camera, tst/underwater.ppm).
- The first main camera's sets `sound_underwater` (src/sounds/set/volume.c): sound effects at 60%, music at 30%,
  and the mixer muffles the whole mix (src/sounds/mix/mixer.c): a 500hz low pass, two one pole stages, eased in and
  out over about 45ms; tst sound_mixer checks a high tone is cut and a low one passes.

## Tests

- `water_block`: placed water in a brick pool, the faces, the blended mesh, its own texture (off the tilemap, on the
  gpu, changing), its mesh linking it and its faces' uvs spanning it.
- `water_tile`: the water texture off the tilemap, a block texture's size, at any Block Res.
- `block_frames`: every animated block's frames side by side (tst/blocks/<block>_frames.ppm), they change, loop and
  tile.
- `water_light`: a pool in sunlight filled a layer at a time, each a light_dim dimmer down; its top drained; covered.
- `water_flow`: no player; a source over a brick floor falls, lands and spreads a level less a block (7 - d, none past
  6), its mesh draws the levels' tops at their heights; a brick dug from under it, it falls down the hole; the source
  taken out, it all drains.
- `swim`: a character dropped in a pool sinks slower than 1m a second, swims up to the surface holding jump (not
  far over it), and climbs out onto a shore; `terrain_water_at` in the pool and over it.
- `chunk_order`: see through chunk meshes drawn far to near, opaque ones as they come.
- `sea_level` and `lakes`: no player, a realm and terrain of their own (src/controllers/tst/world_only.c), one camera
  over the nearest open water or lake, its land streamed round it, looking down (tst/sea.ppm, tst/lake.ppm); its
  water checked against the heights. `zox_tst_sea_near=1` looks at the sea from close, `zox_tst_sea_under=1` from
  under it (tst/sea.ppm shows the underwater fog).

## Not yet

  its side faces' tops full, not sloped.
- Blended faces within one chunk mesh aren't sorted; meshes in different tables aren't sorted against each other.
- Flood light (sideways) through water isn't dimmed more than through air.
- Towns on sunken ground by the sea (todo.md Bugs).
