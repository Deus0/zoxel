# Water

How water works now: the block, how it's drawn, the sea and lakes, and how it's tested.

## The block

- `water` is a realm block (gam/zoxel/sys/blocks.c, `BlockWater`): `BlockMaterial` liquid, `BlockCollider` air
  (walked through), `BlockLightPass` (light goes through it).
- A liquid is a chunk material of its own (chunk materials, src/chunks/chunks3/fun/materials.c): its faces draw only
  against air and other see through materials that aren't water, so there are no faces between water blocks or
  against solids, and a solid keeps its face against water (the sea floor shows through).
- Its chunk meshes are tagged `TransparentMesh` and drawn by `chunk3_transparent_render_system`, after the solid
  ones and the sky, blended, both faces (src/rendering/rendering3/sys/render_chunks.c).

## Its texture

- `prefab_texture_water` (src/textures/pre/noise_texture.c), generated by `water_texture_system`: 16 frames of
  ripples (src/textures/gen/water.c), three waves with whole wave numbers across it and whole cycles over the frames,
  so it tiles and loops; a few shades, the crests brightest.
- Its size is set when its block is made (`spawn_realm_block_textured`), to the size of every block's texture then
  (the Block Res setting resizes the block texture prefabs only).
- The tilemap shows it moving: `TilemapAnimations` lists each animated texture's tile, `tilemap_animate_system`
  copies the frame for the time (`TextureFrameRate`, 8 a second) into the tilemap and marks it dirty.

## The sea

- Ground below `terrain_sea_height` (6, times `render_distance_y`: 18) is under water up to it
  (src/heights/sys/landfill.c). The land is mostly 17 to 30 high, so about a tenth is under it; sand reaches 21 for
  its shores. Vegetation never grows under 21.

## Lakes

- src/heights/fun/_.c: a 64 block cell (`zox_lake_cell`) holds a lake 30% of the time, seeded from the terrain's
  seed: a centre, a radius of 8 to 15, a depth of 3 to 5. Its bowl is carved into the heights
  (`terrain_height_at` = `terrain_height_base` less `terrain_lake_dip`), deepest in the middle.
- Its level is a block under its rim's lowest point (16 samples of the land before carving), so it stays in its bowl;
  one under the sea is just a dip the sea fills.
- Landfill fills water to the higher of the sea and the column's lake (each lake's level worked out once a chunk).
- Nothing grows in a bowl (vegetation skips its columns); towns and mountains keep out of lakes
  (`region_zones_add_lakes` adds them to a region's placement zones before those are placed).

## Flowing

- src/terrain/sys/water_flow.c. A chunk just edited (`VoxelNodePostDirty`) adds its edited blocks to its terrain's
  `WaterFlows`; `water_flow_system` takes up to 64 due a frame:
  - air with water over it or beside it fills with water;
  - water falls into air under it, else spreads to air beside it, at most 4 blocks (`zox_water_flow_steps`) from
    where the flow began; a step every quarter second (`zox_water_flow_time`).
- Flowing water's own edits are marked (`VoxelNodeUpdate.flowed`) and start no flow, so it stops. Dug out of a
  lake's side, a lake fills the hole and spreads 4 into a tunnel; the lake itself never drains (water doesn't move,
  only fills).
- Generated water (landfill) doesn't flow: only edits do.

## Tests

- `water_block`: placed water and bricks, the faces, the blended mesh, the tile animating and filling its tile, its
  faces' uvs spanning it.
- `block_frames`: every animated block's frames side by side (tst/blocks/<block>_frames.ppm), they change, loop and
  tile.
- `water_flow`: no player; water dropped on a brick floor lands and covers the blocks within 4 of where it landed,
  then a brick dug from under it drains it down the hole.
- `sea_level` and `lakes`: no player, a realm and terrain of their own (src/controllers/tst/world_only.c), one camera
  over the nearest open water or lake, its land streamed round it, looking down (tst/sea.ppm, tst/lake.ppm); its
  water checked against the heights. `zox_tst_sea_near=1` looks at the sea from close.
