# Blocks

## Solid Blocks
* They have all 6 sides
- Dirt
- Wood
- Stone

## Transparent Blocks
* Liquid blocks that render over the solids
- Water
- Blood
- Acid

## Vox Blocks
* Made up of vox models, spawned in world
- Flowers
- Mushrooms

- Doors (swings open)
