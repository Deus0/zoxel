# Rewards

- Treasure Chests in dungeons
- Get coins when you finish quest
    - quest accept ui shows coins pile rotating, to motivate you to do it
- visually, combat should feel rewarding, both sounds and particles and animation as you go through hits
- Quest Chains - Unique Items / Titles (those give bonuses)
- Exploration - higher level means you can visit more areas, dungeon types, and cities
- Items - the more crafting you do, you unlock more stuff to play with - higher level = add more content
