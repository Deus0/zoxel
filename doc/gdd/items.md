# Items

- Cookies: Eatable, Findable, Everyone has a cookie stash
- Bombs: can light with fireball on left arm and throw with right (or reverse)
    - throw or place
    - can light and run away
    - blows up cracked walls which are semi hiding in dungeons or around moutnains
- Keys: Open locked doors, like zelda, the chains unlock animation
- Weapons: they are attached to hand, but are not combined, in order to do things like throw them
- Throwing Weapons: They are released and thrown, the character will do a animation, like grabbing another from their backpack
